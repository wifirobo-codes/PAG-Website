Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about PAG and our mushrooms.

PAG, or Perfect Agro Group, was founded in 2025 by Promila Jain and Altab Badami. The company is registered in Gujarat, India, with future plans focused on innovation in agriculture.

Button mushrooms are considered vegetarian food. They are a nutritious food containing protein, vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients.

PAG focuses on providing fresh and carefully cultivated mushrooms. Our mushrooms are supplied soon after harvesting to help maintain their freshness and quality.
